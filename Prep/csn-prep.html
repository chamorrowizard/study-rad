Transfer Fine Print

  • 64-Credit Max Cap: UNLV caps transfer credits from community colleges at 64. Stick strictly to required A.S. electives.
  • Calculus Co-requisites: CS 202 requires MATH 182 (Calc II) either completed or taken concurrently. Plan math sequencing carefully.
  • Grade Standards: Computer Science major requirements at UNLV strictly mandate a **C or better** (C- is not accepted for major core).

Grade Replacement & UNLV Standards

  • UNLV CS Standard ("C" or Better): Core STEM and communication courses (`ENG 102`, `COM 101`, Calculus, CS 135/202) strictly require a **`C` or higher**. Note: Your previous `COM 101` (`C-`) must be retaken for UNLV transfer eligibility.
  • CSN Grade Replacement Strategy: Retaking `ENG 102` & `HIST 101` in Semester 1, and `COM 101` in Semester 2, replaces past attempts in your CSN GPA calculation to maximize your GPA for scholarships.
  • Existing Gen Ed Credits: Your past passing credits in `CHEM 105` (B), `MUS 125` (B+), and `CRJ 270` (A) fulfill key A.S. General Education distribution requirements.
  • Millennium Scholarship Retention: Complete all 15 credits each semester without dropping below full-time status and maintain at least a **2.75 GPA**.

DOE / MSTS Portfolio Benchmarks

Build these key projects during CSN (CS 135/202) to showcase systems proficiency on your resume and GitHub:

  • Low-Level Systems Program (C/C++): Memory management tool, custom shell, or CLI utility compiled natively in Linux/WSL.
  • Network Packet Analyzer (Python/C): Simple socket listener or packet parser demonstrating core OSI model & TCP/IP knowledge.
  • Rust / Systems Safety Demo: Small utility showcasing memory safety without a garbage collector—a massive plus for modern defense dev roles.

Master CSN → UNLV → MSTS Operational Timeline (15-Credit Scholarship Track)

Structured 15-credit/semester schedule maximizing Millennium/Nevada scholarship funding, GPA grade replacement, and CS transfer prep.

Semester 1: Fall (15 Credits) — Math Foundation & GPA Reset

• Courses (15 cr): MATH 126E (3) + MATH 26 Lab (3) | CIT 129 Intro Programming (3) | ENG 102 Composition II (3 - Grade Replacement) | HIST 101 US History I (3 - Grade Replacement)
• Scholarships & VR&E: Confirm Millennium Scholarship / CSN Foundation 15-credit status & secure Chapter 31 tech package.
• Action Item: Focus on replacing past ENG 102 / HIST 101 grades to immediately boost cumulative GPA.

Semester 2: Spring (15 Credits) — Pre-Calculus II & Gen Ed Core

• Courses (15 cr): MATH 127 Pre-Calculus II (3) | COM 101 Oral Communication (3) | PHIL 102 Critical Thinking (3) | Natural Science W/ Lab (4) | Elective/General Ed (2)
• Cert Prep: Begin studying for CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) to build defense/clearance baseline.
• Milestone: Passing MATH 127 unlocks CS 135 (Computer Science I) for Fall.

Semester 3: Fall (15 Credits) — CS 135, Calculus I & DOE Clearance Applications

• Courses (15 cr): CS 135 CS I in C++ (3) | MATH 181 Calculus I (4) | Natural Science W/ Lab (4) | Social Science Elective (3) | CS/STEM Elective (1)
• DOE / MSTS Internships (Sept–Oct): Submit applications for DOE SULI & MSTS Summer Internships (allows 6–9 month clearance processing window).
• Scholarships (Nov 1): Submit DoD SMART Scholarship & CyberCorps (SFS) applications.

Semester 4: Spring (15 Credits) — CS 202, Calculus II & A.S. Graduation

• Courses (15 cr): CS 202 CS II in C++ (3) | MATH 182 Calculus II (4) | Fine Arts Elective (3) | Humanities Elective (3) | STEM Transfer Elective (2)
• A.S. Degree Audit: Apply for CSN Associate of Science graduation (60 credits total; locks in full NSHE general education block transfer to UNLV).
• Clearance Readiness: Complete SF-86 security questionnaire audit ahead of summer MSTS internship onboarding.

Phase 5: UNLV CS Transfer & MSTS Full-Time Career Onboarding

• UNLV Junior/Senior Focus: Transfer into UNLV B.S. in Computer Science with 60 credits accepted; enroll in CS 302 (Data Structures) & CS 370 (Operating Systems).
• MSTS Internship & Capstone: Complete Nevada National Security Site (NNSS) summer internship with active DOE Q clearance.
• Career Transition: Convert internship into a full-time software developer role with DOE/MSTS upon UNLV graduation.

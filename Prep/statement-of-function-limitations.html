Statement of Functional Limitations & Vocational Feasibility

Veteran Name: Justin Cabrera
Combined Disability Rating: 100% Permanent & Total (P&T)

1. Prior Work Environment vs. Service-Connected Conditions

My prior employment as an X-Ray Technologist at Summerlin Hospital involved sustained physical exertion, rapid pacing, heavy patient handling, and high-acuity clinical environments. Following my service-connected disability determinations, my service-connected conditions create distinct physical and psychological employment handicaps that render clinical radiology and heavy manual labor medically unviable.

Left Shoulder Degenerative Joint Disease (s/p Clavicle Fracture w/ ORIF) — 20%
  • Functional Limitations: Overhead reaching restrictions, repetitive pushing/pulling limitations, and weight-bearing caps on lifting heavy equipment.
  • Impact on Prior Role: Unable to position heavy overhead X-ray tubes, maneuver 500+ lb mobile fluoroscopy/C-arm units, or assist non-ambulatory hospital patients without triggering joint instability and severe pain.
Status Post Right Tibia Fracture w/ ORIF — 10%
  • Functional Limitations: Prolonged weight-bearing intolerance, lower extremity stiffness, and pain exacerbated by continuous standing or walking.
  • Impact on Prior Role: Unable to endure 8-to-12-hour hospital shifts spent on hard flooring without triggering acute lower leg inflammation and severe fatigue.
Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) — 100% P&T
  • Functional Limitations: Vulnerability to high-sensory, chaotic, or unpredictable environments; cognitive fatigue under acute environmental stress.
  • Impact on Prior Role: High-acuity hospital environments (trauma bays, emergency codes, alarms) serve as continuous environmental triggers, compromising long-term focus and psychological stability.

2. Vocational Feasibility of Software Engineering

Transitioning to a Software Engineering career directly resolves my employment handicaps by removing physical hazards and providing a controlled, sustainable work setting:

Occupational Aspect Radiologic Technologist (Prior) Software Engineer (Target)
Physical Workload Heavy lifting, pushing equipment, patient transfers. Sedentary, desk-based work with zero heavy lifting.
Postural Requirement Continuous standing/walking (8–12 hours). Alternating sit/stand posture via electric desk.
Sensory Environment Chaotic, high-noise hospital & ER conditions. Structured, predictable, low-sensory environment.
Flexibility & Controls Rigid shift hours; physical presence required. Ergonomic adaptabilities, hybrid/remote potential.
Summary & Recommendation
My service-connected physical and mental conditions prevent a return to Radiologic Technology. Retraining in Computer Science / Software Engineering addresses all functional limitations by establishing an ergonomic, desk-based, and psychologically stable career path aligned with my vocational capabilities.
